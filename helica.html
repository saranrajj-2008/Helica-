Process

Four steps. Zero traffic.

01 / BOOK

Set your route

Enter pickup and destination. Helica quotes an instant fare based on distance and passenger count.

Confirm the window, aircraft, and pilot assigned to your flight. You get live status from that point on.

03 / BOARD

Walk to the pad

Arrive minutes before departure. No terminals, no queues — just a short walk to the helipad.

Land above it all

Direct point-to-point flight, well above the traffic that would've taken hours on the ground.
